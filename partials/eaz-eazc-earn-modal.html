Sales earnings

How you earn EAZC

EAZC is credited when someone actually buys a product. This page covers sales earnings only — not daily game coins.

New sales EAZC stays pending for 30 days (return window), then it becomes available to cash out or convert.

01 Your products You sell a design you published.

You receive your royalty share of the net profit (10–40%). Community cashback and referrals are paid from the separate community share.

02 Remixes Someone remixed your design — or you remixed theirs.

From the creator share: the remix seller receives 87.5% and the original creator receives 12.5%.

03 Quick Inspirations Someone sold a design made from your Quick Inspiration.

The Quick Inspiration author receives 5 royalty points from the seller’s creator share (half of that share at the 10% starter royalty). Automatic trend inspirations do not pay.

04 Shop designs Your design is used on a shop or custom product.

Up to 3 designs on one product share the creator amount equally. If a slot is a remix, that slot still splits 87.5% / 12.5%.

05 Creator Community You and your recruiter both opted in.

The seller keeps 70% of the creator share. The recruiter who invited them receives 30%. Publish Assist does not add an extra split.

06 Referrals & cashback Someone buys with your link — or you buy while logged in.

From the community share: the buyer receives 20% cashback, your direct referral link earns 10%, and further network levels earn smaller shares.

Your creator share starts at 10% of net profit and can rise to 40% in Creator Journey → Royalties.
